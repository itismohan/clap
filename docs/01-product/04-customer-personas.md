# Customer personas

Personas are **roles at the Fortune 100 tenant** plus **consumers of the brand**. Names are fictional.

## P1 — Maya Chen, VP CRM & Lifecycle (champion)

- **Goals:** Lift retention and acquisition without increasing discount rate; prove incrementality to the CMO.
- **Pains:** Four tools for offers, audiences, email, and reporting; 3-week campaign QA.
- **Jobs in CLAP:** Approve offer calendar, set frequency caps, read promo ROI, use copilot for “who should get this.”
- **Success:** Fewer tools, faster launches, clear holdout results.

## P2 — Jordan Hale, Campaign Manager

- **Goals:** Ship weekly campaigns on brand, on time.
- **Pains:** Spreadsheet eligibility, last-minute legal edits, no view of in-app decisioning.
- **Jobs:** Build audiences, attach offers, schedule email + in-app, monitor send and take-rate.
- **Success:** Launch in days, not weeks; alerts on underperforming offers.

## P3 — Priya Nair, Offer / Merchandising Lead

- **Goals:** Move seasonal and partner inventory without stacking conflicts.
- **Pains:** Two coupons stacking to negative margin; expired offers still live on site.
- **Jobs:** Offer catalog, eligibility, budget, inventory, stacking rules, calendar.
- **Success:** One catalog; no live offer without budget and end date.

## P4 — Alex Okonkwo, CMO (economic buyer)

- **Goals:** Efficient growth; board-ready story on promo spend.
- **Pains:** “We spent $X on promotions” with no incremental narrative.
- **Jobs:** Executive scorecard, quarterly business review with CLAP metrics.
- **Success:** Incremental customers and ROAS of promo, not vanity open rates.

## P5 — Samira El-Sayed, Consumer (brand app user)

- **Goals:** Relevant deals without spam.
- **Pains:** Irrelevant coupons, too many emails, offers that fail at checkout.
- **Jobs:** Sees personalized offer inbox, homepage module, checkout offer; manages preferences.
- **Success:** Offers that work, easy opt-down, trust.

## P6 — Diego Martins, Tenant Engineer

- **Goals:** Stable SDK and APIs; no PII leaks.
- **Pains:** Batch file drops, undocumented events.
- **Jobs:** Decisioning API, webhooks, SSO, sandbox.
- **Success:** OpenAPI, SLOs, runbooks.

## P7 — Riley Cho, Promo Abuse / Fraud Analyst

- **Goals:** Stop coupon farming and fake referrals without blocking good customers.
- **Jobs:** Velocity rules, case queue, allow/deny lists.
- **Success:** Fraud savings vs. false positives.

## P8 — Legal / Privacy counsel (blocker turned partner)

- **Goals:** Lawful marketing, auditable consent, approval of copy.
- **Jobs:** Preference center, purpose limitation, campaign legal workflow.
- **Success:** Every send has a legal basis and an approval record.
