# Market research

## Category definition

**Enterprise promotional personalization:** software that (a) governs offers, (b) decides which customer sees which promotion in real time or near real time, and (c) orchestrates delivery across owned channels, with measurement.

Adjacent spend: CDP, marketing cloud, merchandising, coupon services, experimentation platforms.

## Demand drivers (F100)

- Shift from blast campaigns to **in-session** experiences (app homepage, cart).
- Pressure on **promo margin** as consumers wait for discounts.
- Privacy: cookies deprecation, consent, first-party data activation.
- Generative AI expectation: faster copy and campaign drafting with controls.
- Procurement: consolidate point tools (coupon + email + on-site) if TCO is clear.

## Buyer research hypotheses (validate in interviews)

1. Offer eligibility is still Excel or commerce-admin only.
2. Personalization teams own models; CRM teams own email—handoff is the gap.
3. Incrementality is requested by finance and rarely productionized.
4. Brands will not rip out Adobe / Salesforce / Braze in year one; they will add decisioning and catalog.

## Channel mix for “attract and promote”

| Channel | Role in CLAP |
| --- | --- |
| Web / app | Real-time decisioning (highest priority) |
| Email / push / SMS | Campaign promotion of selected offers |
| Paid media | Audience export (phase 2) |
| Associates / store | Offer codes (phase 2) |

## Vertical patterns (same engine, different offer types)

- **Retail / marketplace:** coupons, bundles, free ship, partner deals  
- **QSR / CPG:** geo, daypart, combo  
- **Telco / streaming:** plan upgrade, trial, win-back  
- **Travel:** fare/ancillary offers, member rates as offer objects  

Fintech-specific products (credit cards as SKUs) are an **offer type pack**, not the platform identity.

## Barriers to adoption

- Identity resolution quality  
- Real-time feature availability  
- Legal review of automated copy  
- Agency of record lock-in  
- Fear of “black box” ranking
